About

I am a music producer and sound engineer living in Lisbon, Portugal. 

I do quite a wide range of activities in sound: creating music from idea to finished product, including recording, arranging, mixing, mastering and distribution stages, as well as sound design for commercials, podcasts and shows.

I work with various artists all over the world, both professionals and amateurs, music labels and production companies in Europe and USA.
