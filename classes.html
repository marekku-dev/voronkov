Production classes (it's actually easier than you think)

I've taken a lot of courses and training myself, and I've realized that the role of the teacher is actually crucial. As is the goal you are pursuing.

Do you sing or play an instrument and would you like to learn how to record yourself and be able to make arrangements of your songs? With me you will master this process in one month.

Do you want to learn music production and become a professional producer? I will gladly help you on this path, show you all the hidden problems and warn you against the mistakes I made myself.

Or maybe you have nothing to do with music but would like to try to write a beat or a whole song? I am convinced that the process of creating music is one of the most amazing and addictive things in life. It is a process of meditation, self-discovery and total immersion into the amazing world of sound that you are building.

Every time I produce it's like I'm not even on this planet. Imagine being able to experience that feeling anytime you want. All you need is a laptop, headphones and a few lessons with me.
